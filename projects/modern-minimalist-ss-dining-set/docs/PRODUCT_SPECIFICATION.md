# Product Specification — SDS-100 Dining Set

## 1. Overview
Indoor four-seat dining set in a modern minimalist style. Designed for domestic dining use.

## 2. Dining table — SDS-T120
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Overall dimensions | 1200 mm (L) × 750 mm (W) × 750 mm (H) |
| Top | 12 mm tempered safety glass (EN 12150), reverse-printed Calacatta marble pattern, polished flat edge, 2 mm arris |
| Top overhang | 40 mm on all sides |
| Frame outer size | 1120 × 670 mm (leg centres 1090 × 640 mm) |
| Frame | AISI 304 (EN 1.4301) square tube 30 × 30 × 1.5 mm; 4 legs, top rail frame (underside of glass at 738 mm), lower stretcher frame at 110–140 mm |
| Construction | TIG butt-welded, tube ends capped, welds dressed flush, mirror polished (Ra ≤ 0.1 µm, #8) |
| Top fixing | Neutral-cure structural silicone; 4 clear PU corner bumpers |
| Feet | M8 stainless levelling feet, ±8 mm adjustment |
| Mass | Frame 13.25 kg, top 26.14 kg, total 39.4 kg |

## 3. Dining chair — SDS-C45
| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Overall dimensions | 430 mm (W) × 483 mm (D) × 900 mm (H) |
| Seat height | 450 mm; seat depth 414–420 mm; seat/back thickness 40 mm |
| Back | 450 mm above seat, 8° rake |
| Legs | Tapered square tube, 30 × 30 mm at top to 20 × 20 mm at foot, 410 mm long, 1.5 mm wall, end-capped, AISI 304 |
| Leg spacing | 360 mm (width) × 360 mm (depth), centres |
| Seat sub-frame | 20 × 20 × 1.5 mm AISI 304 tube, welded to legs |
| Seat and back | 12 mm moulded plywood core, 28 mm HR polyurethane foam (40 kg/m³), woven fabric (≥ 50,000 Martindale cycles), R8 edge radius |
| Fixing | 6 × M6 stainless fasteners from sub-frame to shell |
| Feet | Nylon glides |
| Mass | Steel frame 3.07 kg; chair approx. 6.8 kg |

## 4. Set arrangement
- Chair centres 470 mm; seat front tucked 45 mm under table edge.
- Seat-to-table clearance 300 mm; clearance under frame rail 258 mm.
- Footprint with chairs pushed in: 1200 × 1624 mm. Recommended clear room 3.0 × 2.6 m.

## 5. Materials
| Item | Material / finish |
|---|---|
| Frames (table and chair) | AISI 304 stainless steel, mirror polished #8 |
| Table top | Tempered glass, marble-pattern reverse print |
| Chair upholstery | Grey woven fabric |
| Chair core | Moulded plywood + HR PU foam |

## 6. Reference standards for production
EN 12521 (domestic tables), EN 12520 (domestic seating), EN 12150 (tempered glass), EN 10296-2 (welded stainless tube), ISO 2768-m (general tolerances).

## 7. Care
Clean steel with a soft damp cloth and mild detergent; wipe dry. Glass: standard glass cleaner. Fabric: vacuum, spot-clean with a damp cloth.
