# Manufacturing and Assembly Notes — SDS-100

## Table frame
1. Cut AISI 304 30 × 30 × 1.5 tube: 4 legs at 738 mm, 4 long rails at 1060 mm, 4 short rails at 610 mm (square cut, deburred).
2. Fit up on a flat jig: legs at 1090 × 640 mm centres; top rails flush with the leg tops (rail zone 708–738 mm); lower stretchers at 110–140 mm.
3. TIG butt-weld all joints; cap tube ends; grind welds flush.
4. Polish to #8 mirror finish; passivate.
5. Fit M8 levelling feet.

## Table top
Cut, polish edges, temper and reverse-print the 12 mm glass. Bond to the frame with structural silicone; fit four corner bumpers; leave a uniform 40 mm overhang.

## Chair frame
1. Form tapered tube legs (30 → 20 mm, 410 mm, 1.5 mm wall) and cap the ends.
2. Cut four 20 × 20 × 1.5 seat rails at 332 mm.
3. Weld legs and rails in a jig at 360 × 360 mm leg centres, rail zone 390–410 mm.
4. Polish to #8 and passivate.

## Seat and back
Mould the 12 mm plywood core as a continuous L-profile (8° rake, 430 wide), laminate 28 mm HR foam, upholster in fabric, fit R8 edges. Fix to the sub-frame with 6 × M6 fasteners; fit nylon glides.

## Quality checks
| Check | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Overall dimensions | Within ISO 2768-m |
| Table top flatness | Even 40 mm overhang, glass seated without rocking |
| Weld finish | Flush, no porosity, polished to #8 |
| Chair seat height | 450 mm |
| Chair rake | 8° ± 1° |
| Upholstery | No creases at edges; fabric ≥ 50,000 Martindale |
