# Product Specification — Outdoor Telecom Cabinet System

Dimensions in inches (millimetres in brackets) unless noted.

## 1. System overview

A two-cabinet outdoor power and equipment solution:

- **RAN equipment cabinet** — houses customer equipment on 19" rails together with a 12 kW DC power system, AC distribution and backup battery string 1.
- **External battery cabinet** — houses backup battery strings 2 and 3 with DC distribution.

### Cooling and heating
- Direct-air cooling tracks outside temperature, inside temperature and the difference between them (Delta T)
- Front door: louvered intake hood with two filter panels and two fans
- Rear door: slotted exhaust hood with filter and fan
- Heating: door-mounted heater element with fan, on/off operation in fixed modes
- Battery compartment fan speed steps by outside temperature:

| Outside temperature | Fan speed |
|---|---|
| 15–18 °C | 2000 RPM |
| 19–29 °C | 3000 RPM |
| 30–48 °C | 4800 RPM |
| Above 48 °C | 5800 RPM |

### DC power system (12 kW)
- 6-position rectifier shelf
- Controller with display
- Dual PDUs, each with independent low-voltage disconnect (LVD)
  - Critical PDU: 22 positions, 14 populated with 20 A breakers
  - Non-critical PDU: 22 positions, 14 populated with 20 A breakers
  - 44 breaker positions total, up to 30 A each
- DC surge arrestors

### AC power
- Input voltage range: 208–240 VAC
- Maximum feed: 100 A
- Short-circuit rating: 10 kAIC
- AC load center with AC surge protection

### Alarms and sensors
Monitoring for door intrusion (front and rear), clogged filter, DC power, cabinet environment and tower light, with two spare inputs; battery thermal probe.

### Internal battery
- String 1: four VRLA blocks on stacked trays with interconnects
