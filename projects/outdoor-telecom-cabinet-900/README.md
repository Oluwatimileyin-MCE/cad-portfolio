# Outdoor Telecom Cabinet 900 x 900 x 2060

Outdoor telecom equipment cabinet modelled in Autodesk Fusion: a galvanised-steel enclosure on a hoistable plinth with a hinged, lockable door, a 30U 19-inch rack, a battery compartment, a 1500 W side-mounted air conditioner and a full set of monitoring sensors. The assembly is fully jointed, so the door swings open on its three hinges and every internal part is positioned and constrained.

![Isometric, front left](docs/images/01-isometric-front-left.png)

## Highlights

- 27 components, 36 instances, 122 bodies, with 34 rigid joints and 3 revolute hinge joints (door opens 0 to 110 degrees)
- 2 mm galvanised-steel body, hip roof canopy, door gasket and two louvred ventilation windows
- Plinth with 4 x dia 40 hoisting holes, 4 x dia 18 anchor holes and a rear cable-entry opening
- 30U 19-inch rack with standard mounting-hole pattern, three equipment shelves, DC distribution, rectifier shelf, fibre patch panel and monitoring unit
- Battery compartment with tray and four 12 V 100 Ah batteries
- 1500 W air conditioner with wall ports, fan and louvres
- Smoke sensor, thermostat, water-ingress sensor, entrance guard, 48 V LED lamp and copper grounding bar
- Interference-checked with the door closed and at 90 and 110 degrees
