# Start here: 22U Pyramid-Canopy Outdoor Street Cabinet

**One-page guide for contractors and reviewers**

| You want to | Open |
|---|---|

## Status

Complete design release, Rev A: parametric 3D model, two-sheet dimensioned drawing set, specification, parts list and multi-angle model views.

## Key facts

- 22U rack capacity (977.9 mm mounting height)
- Body 814 x 462 x 1578 mm, overall height 1728 mm with the pyramid canopy
- 2 mm steel sheet body, RAL 7035 light grey powder coat, IP56
- Front lockable door and side door with internal lock
- 4 feeder and 48 secondary cable entries in the base plate
