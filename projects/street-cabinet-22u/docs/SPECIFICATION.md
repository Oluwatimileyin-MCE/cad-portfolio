# Specification: 22U Pyramid-Canopy Outdoor Street Cabinet

Drawing set SC22U-GA-001 and 002, Rev A. All dimensions in millimetres. General tolerance ISO 2768-m.

## 1. Application

Outdoor fibre-optic distribution cabinet for fibre telecommunication networks and FTTx. Provides fixation of optical cables, splicing, termination, storage and management of fibres, and fixing and cross-connection of optical fibres.
