# Assembly and operation

## Assembly sequence

1. **Strap:** stretch the strap ring (relaxed 50 mm inside diameter) over the neck thread and seat it on the neck root between the thread and the shoulder step. It then turns freely on the neck.
2. **Ring A:** pass ring A through the 5.0 mm hole in the strap eye.
3. **Ring B:** pass ring B through the 5.0 mm hole in the cap lug.
4. **Link:** interlink ring A and ring B.
5. **Gasket:** press the gasket into the recess in the cap underside, with 1.0 mm protruding.
6. **Close:** place the cap on the neck and turn it clockwise until the gasket seats.

## Opening

1. Turn the cap counter-clockwise. It rises 9.0 mm per turn, and the strap ring turns with it.
2. At 14.5 mm (580 deg, about 1.6 turns) the cap is free of the thread.
3. Lift slightly and swing the cap 180 deg about its lug hole. It hangs on the link rings, clear of the mouth.

## Closing

1. Swing the cap back over the neck on its link rings.
2. Turn it clockwise. It descends 9.0 mm per turn, and the strap ring turns with it.
3. Continue until the gasket seats on the flat neck land (0.5 mm squeeze). The thread provides clamp load; the gasket makes the seal.

The thread has three starts spaced 120 deg, so the cap finds engagement within a third of a turn.

| Cap rotation | Cap lift |
|---|---|
| 0 deg (closed) | 0.0 mm |
| 180 deg | 4.5 mm |
| 360 deg | 9.0 mm |
| 580 deg | 14.5 mm (free of thread) |
