Drawing set OTC-23RU-001 to 004, Rev A. All dimensions in millimetres. General tolerance ISO 2768-m. Datums: X = left edge of cabinet (front view), Y = rear face, Z = ground plane.

Pole-mounted outdoor telecom cabinet with a 23RU, 19-inch open rack for network equipment, with a monopole side access port for cable entry.

## 14. Fabrication notes

1. Material: galvanised carbon-steel sheet (SGCC) for all 1.5 mm parts; thicknesses as in the bill of materials.
2. Cut profiles by laser or punch; deburr all edges and break sharp edges to 0.3 max.
3. Form bends per the bend schedule; bend lines perpendicular to rolling direction where practicable.
4. Weld per the weld schedule (ISO 2553 symbols, ISO 5817 level C); grind external seams flush before coating.
5. Pre-treat (degrease and phosphate), then powder coat: polyester, RAL 7035, 60 to 100 micron dry film.
6. Dimensions are finished sizes after coating. The front door fits the opening with a nominal 3 mm gap all round.
7. Rail holes: dia 7.1, EIA-310 pattern; rail faces coplanar within 1 mm across the four rails.
8. Mark item number and revision on each fabricated part. First-article check: overall size, rail hole pattern, door fit.
