# Specification

## 1. General requirements

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Units | Millimetres |
| General tolerances | ISO 2768-m |
| Projection | ISO first angle |
| Surface finish (general) | Ra 3.2 um; functional bores and spigots Ra 1.6 um |
| Edge treatment | Break sharp edges 0.5 mm max; deburr all holes |
| Thread standard | ISO metric coarse, M12 x 1.75; studs 6g, nuts and tapped holes 6H |

## 2. Assembly

- Shaft: Ø24 mm, passes through the bush, packing chamber and gland.
- Packing chamber: Ø42 mm bore from the top face down to 9 mm above the base, then Ø33 mm through the base. Packing seats on the bush collar and is compressed by the gland spigot.
- Fastening: two M12 studs at 84 mm centres screw into the body top flange; two M12 nuts bear on the gland flange.
- Gland adjustment: nominal gap between gland flange and body top face is 13 mm; mechanical travel +5 mm / -8 mm; recommended working travel about 4 mm.
